Badminton — Quick Guide

Badminton is an exciting and fast-paced sport enjoyed by people around the world. Here is a quick guide using lists!

Steps to Become a Better Badminton Player:

  1. Practice your footwork regularly
  2. Warm up before every match
  3. Learn proper racket grip techniques
  4. Stay hydrated during practice sessions
  5. Play matches often to improve confidence

Famous Badminton Achievements:

  • P.V. Sindhu — first Indian woman to win an Olympic silver medal in badminton
  • Saina Nehwal — one of India's top badminton champions
  • Lin Dan — known as one of the greatest badminton players in history
  • Viktor Axelsen — Olympic champion and world-class badminton player

Key Terms:

Shuttlecock
The object players hit back and forth during a badminton match
Racket
The equipment used to hit the shuttlecock during the game
Smash
A powerful and fast shot used to score points in badminton
