🍕 Why Your Body Needs Healthy Food

Your body is like a machine — the better food you eat, the better it works! People who eat healthy meals every day feel more energetic, stay active, and remain healthy.

Pizza, burgers, fruits, salads, and homemade meals — enjoying a balance of tasty and healthy food is important. Eat well and stay happy!

🍎 Quick Food Tips

  • 🍎 Eat fresh fruits every day
  • 💧 Drink enough water daily
  • 🍰 Enjoy desserts in moderation
  • 🍔 Try homemade meals whenever possible
