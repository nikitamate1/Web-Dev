Badminton is a fast-paced racket sport played between two players or two teams of two players each. The game is played using rackets and a shuttlecock, which players hit back and forth across a net. The main objective is to score points by landing the shuttlecock in the opponent's court. Badminton helps improve fitness, reflexes, concentration, and coordination. It is enjoyed by people of all ages and is played both recreationally and professionally around the world.
