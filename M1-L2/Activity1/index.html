🏸 Fact 1: The Fastest Racket Sport

Badminton is one of the fastest racket sports in the world. A shuttlecock can travel at speeds over 400 km/h during powerful smashes.

Why is badminton so fast?

The lightweight shuttlecock and quick player movements make badminton a very fast and exciting game to watch and play.

⚡ Fact 2: Olympic Sport

Badminton became an official Olympic sport in 1992. Players from around the world compete to win medals and represent their countries.

Who are famous badminton players?

Players like P.V. Sindhu, Saina Nehwal, and Lin Dan are known for their incredible achievements in badminton.

Can badminton improve fitness?

Yes! Playing badminton improves stamina, reflexes, balance, and overall physical fitness.

🌟 Fact 3: Shuttlecock Fun

A badminton shuttlecock is made using feathers or synthetic material and is specially designed for smooth flight during matches.

How many feathers are used?

A professional badminton shuttlecock usually contains 16 feathers carefully arranged for balanced movement.

Fun comparison

A badminton smash can travel faster than many sports balls, making it one of the quickest sports actions in the world.
